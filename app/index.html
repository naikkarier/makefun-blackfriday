How we check a deal

Every price on this page was read on 27 Nov 2026 from a deals roundup that names the store, and each card says which roundup it came from. The verdict on each deal is our honest call:

  • NEW LOW: the lowest price recorded for that product in 2026.
  • STRONG DEAL: a deep cut on a current product, well below its usual price.
  • GOOD DEAL: a real saving, though not the deepest cut of the sale.
  • ROUTINE SALE: a modest cut. Fine if you already planned to buy it, not a reason to buy it.

Store links open the store's own search for the exact product. Prices move by the hour during Black Friday week, so always check the live price before you pay.
